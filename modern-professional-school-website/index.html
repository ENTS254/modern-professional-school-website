Welcome to Our School

Enkakenya Ng'ejuk
Testimony School

"Where Every Sunrise Sparks Potential"

At Enkakenya Ng'ejuk Testimony School, we are committed to providing every child with a strong academic foundation while nurturing their character, creativity, and confidence. Located in the heart of Suswa Town, our school is a beacon of hope and opportunity for families in our community.

We follow the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), ensuring that our learners develop practical skills, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning. Our qualified and dedicated teachers create a warm, safe, and stimulating environment where children thrive.
